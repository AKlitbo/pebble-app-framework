Detailed Description

The fetch timing the polling stores share: the one-shot catch-up, the cadence turn, and what an init or a reconfigure does to them.

Each store keeps one StoreFetch and points it at its own request. The interval and deadline decisions stay in store_poll.h, so everything here is a guard around a timer or a request.
