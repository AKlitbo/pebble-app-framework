    Every cap and marker value the two sides of a strip have to agree on.

    The watch reads these back out of c/core/wire/wire_caps.g.h, which npm run build:conditions writes from this table. They are the numbers a decode is bounds-checked against, so a pair that drifted would truncate a strip or refuse a whole message with nothing to show for it.

    A text cap here counts characters. The C side gets that plus one, because its buffer has to hold a terminator as well.
