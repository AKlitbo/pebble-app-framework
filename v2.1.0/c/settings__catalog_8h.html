Detailed Description

The shared catalog of known settings. A KNOWN_* macro per setting bakes in its neutral wire key, encoding, bounds, default, and side effects.

A face subscribes to a known setting by placing its KNOWN_* entry in the face's field table at the offset of the matching struct member, and by declaring the setting's MESSAGE_KEY in the face's package.json. Face-only settings are declared inline in the table instead, with an id of SETTING_COUNT or greater.
