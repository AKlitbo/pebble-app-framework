Detailed Description

The AppMessage pipeline to the phone and the byte readers behind it.

The stores use the byte readers to unpack the packed strips the phone sends.

◆ WeatherHandler

typedef void(*) WeatherHandler(int temp, const char *condition)

Called with a fresh temperature and condition, or with a failed fetch.

The pipeline does not know the unit label. The face adds "°C" or "°F" from its own setting when it draws.

Parameters
tempThe reading in the user's unit, clamped to -99 to 199. Not meaningful when condition is NULL.
conditionThe condition token, or NULL when the fetch failed (no live reading).
