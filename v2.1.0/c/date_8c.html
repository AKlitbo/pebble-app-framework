◆ raw_week()

int raw_week ( int yday,
int wday )
static

The week number worked out from the date alone, before the year boundary is taken into account.

A number below 1 means the day belongs to last year's final week. A number above the year's own total means it already belongs to next year's week 1. The ISO weekday used here runs Monday as 1 through Sunday as 7.

Parameters
ydayThe day of the year counting January 1st as 0 (like tm_yday).
wdayThe weekday, 0 Sunday to 6 Saturday.
Returns
The raw week number, which may fall outside 1 to the year's own week count.
